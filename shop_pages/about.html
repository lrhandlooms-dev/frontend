THE ARTISAN VILLAGE

Where Every Thread Tells a Story

Maniabandha is globally celebrated as a historic hub of Buddhist and Vaishnavite textile traditions. At LR Handlooms, our mission is to empower local weaver communities by sustaining authentic pit-loom craftsmanship without shortcuts.

Every sari takes weeks—sometimes months—of meticulous tie-dyeing and synchronized rhythm on wooden looms to emerge as a timeless heirloom piece.
